Smart Emergency Response System

01 / 04

The Problem

Emergency response today relies on a phone call and a location pin. No way for the caller or the responding vehicle to track each other, and no fast way to know the shortest route between them.

What I Did

Built a C# WinForms app that plots emergency and station locations on Google Maps, calculates the shortest route between them using Dijkstra's algorithm (chosen for non-negative edge weights), and displays live distance and route data, backed by a MySQL database.

What Came Of It

Finished and tested end-to-end. The app correctly displayed routes, distances, and station names for real coordinate inputs. Demoed to my instructor.

Online Food Order System

02 / 04

The Problem

Desktop apps built in WinForms don't naturally support the rich visual experience food ordering needs. Most course projects in this space end up looking like plain data-entry forms with no appetite appeal.

What I Did

Designed a warm purple color palette and placed food images directly in the menu to make the app visually appetizing despite WinForms' limitations. Built full CRUD with multiple restaurants and menus, and enforced a deliberate rule, one restaurant per order, to keep the ordering logic clean and realistic.

What Came Of It

Finished end-to-end through payment processing, tested fully, and demoed to my instructor.

Smart School Management System

03 / 04

What I Did

Designed the full MySQL schema and ERD to handle multiple roles and built the WinForms UI to manage them. Ended up leading and doing most of the implementation myself as a group project, after coordination with teammates didn't work out the way I'd hoped.

What Came Of It

Finished and demoed to my professor. A laptop failure the night before evaluation forced a last-minute switch to another machine with a version mismatch, so we presented a slightly earlier version of the project, still functional, and evaluated successfully.
